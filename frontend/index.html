संसदParliament of India

Lok Sabha (House of the People) is the lower house of Parliament. It has a maximum of 552 elected members — 530 from states, 20 from UTs, and 2 Anglo-Indian nominees (abolished in 2020). Current strength: 543. Speaker: Om Birla. Sessions: Budget (Feb–May), Monsoon (Jul–Aug), Winter (Nov–Dec). Quorum: 10% of total members. Bills: Money bills originate here only.
Rajya Sabha (Council of States) is the upper house. Maximum 250 members: 238 elected by state legislative assemblies + 12 nominated by President for Arts, Science, Literature, Social Service. Rajya Sabha is a permanent body — never dissolved. 1/3rd members retire every 2 years. Chairman: VP Jagdeep Dhankhar. Rajya Sabha can reject or amend non-money bills.
Joint Session (Article 108): called by President when Lok Sabha passes a bill that Rajya Sabha rejects. Chaired by Lok Sabha Speaker. Notable joint sessions: Dowry Prohibition Act 1961, Banking Service Commission (Repeal) Act 1977, Prevention of Terrorism Act 2002. Constitutional amendments require 2/3rd majority in both houses. No-confidence motion: only in Lok Sabha.

मतदाता गाइडComplete Voter's Guide

1
Check Voter Eligibility
You must be an Indian citizen, 18 years or older on January 1 of the revision year, and a resident of the constituency.
2
Register on NVSP / Voter Portal
Apply through nvsp.in or voterportal.eci.gov.in using Form 6. You can also register via the Voter Helpline App.
→ voters.eci.gov.in
3
Get Your EPIC Card
Elector's Photo Identity Card (EPIC) is issued by ECI. Download the e-EPIC from the voter portal.
4
Find Your Polling Booth
Search on electoralsearch.eci.gov.in or SMS “EPIC <voter ID>” to 1950 to find your polling station.
→ electoralsearch.eci.gov.in
5
Vote on Election Day
Carry an approved ID. Polls open 7 AM – 6 PM. Press the button next to your candidate on the EVM. Ink mark on left index finger confirms your vote.
